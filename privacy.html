Privacy policy

Nauczator, Android app. Last updated: 20 November 2026.

In short

Everything the app stores stays on the phone it is used on, and, if a parent chooses, in that parent’s own Google Drive. Nothing is sent to the app’s maker or to anyone else. There is no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

  • On the phone: study sessions (task, subject, times), prizes, settings, the parent password in a protected, scrambled form (it cannot be read back), and the proof of work the child adds: photos, videos and voice recordings.
  • Backups on the phone: the app saves backup files in the phone’s Documents/Nauczator folder.
  • Backups in Google Drive (optional): only if a parent connects their Google account in the password-protected parent area. The backup files are then copied to a “Nauczator” folder in that parent’s own Google Drive.

The camera and microphone are used only when the child takes a photo, records a video or records reading as proof of work, or records an optional short voice note. Nothing is recorded in the background.

Google user data

When a parent connects Google Drive, the app asks Google for one permission only: “See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app” (the drive.file scope). With it, the app:

  • creates a “Nauczator” folder in the parent’s Drive and uploads the app’s backup files there;
  • removes older backup files that the app itself created, so only the newest few are kept;
  • reads the Google account’s email address, only to show the parent which account is connected.

The app cannot see, open or change any other file in the Drive. The access permission and the email address are kept on the phone only. They are not sent to the app’s maker or to anyone else, not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Nauczator’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is meant to be set up by a parent for their child. The child does not create an account and does not give any personal information to anyone. Connecting Google Drive happens only in the parent area, behind the parent password.

Sharing

The app does not share data with anyone. A parent may choose to send a backup or a statistics file themselves, using the phone’s “Send / share” option; where it goes is the parent’s own choice.

How to delete your data

  • On the phone: uninstall the app. This deletes everything the app stores inside itself. Backup files in Documents/Nauczator can be deleted with the phone’s Files app.
  • In Google Drive: delete the “Nauczator” folder.
  • Remove the app’s access to Google Drive: in the app, parent area → Backups → “Disconnect Google Drive”, or at myaccount.google.com/connections.
