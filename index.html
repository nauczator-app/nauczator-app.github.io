Nauczator

A study app for children, made by a parent.

What it is

Nauczator helps children study with a little fun on the side. The child picks a task, studies for a timed block, shows proof of the work (a photo, a video or a reading recording), and then plays a prize lottery with a cartoon animation. A parent area, protected by a password, shows statistics, lets the parent check the proof, and manages prizes and settings.

The app is free, has no ads, and has no user accounts. It is made by a parent for family and friends, for Android.

Backups and Google Drive

The app keeps everything on the phone and saves backups there automatically. A parent can also connect their own Google Drive, and the backups are then copied to a “Nauczator” folder in that Drive. The app can only see the files it creates there, nothing else in the Drive.
